What this is for

The comparison page tells you where your rate sits against the market; the Repricing page turns that into a proposed rate per room and, when you press Apply (or switch it to automatic), signs in here and sets it.

The password is stored encrypted under the same key as the booking-site logins, and no page or API ever returns it. The link, client number and username are shown back so you can see what is set.

One login per account. If the application holds more than one of your properties, they are all reached through this one login.
