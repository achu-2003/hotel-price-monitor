The rule

The market figure is the median of every competitor's entry price in the same tier. The proposal is that figure moved by the position, then capped by the step, then held if outside the floor or ceiling. Percentages are of our current price for the room.

Room mapping

Which row of RMS each of your rooms is. The booking site and RMS name rooms differently (Booking.com's "Deluxe Double Room" is RMS's CLASSIC), so a repricer that guessed would write the right number on the wrong room. Leave a rate row blank if that plan is not sold on the channel; floor and ceiling are RMS rates.
