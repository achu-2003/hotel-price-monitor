The rule

{% if benchmark is defined and benchmark %}

You price against {{ benchmark.name }}. For each of your rooms the rule takes their entry price for the same tier — their cheapest bookable room of that kind, on the same site your own price comes from{% if settings.benchmark_with_tax %}, tax included, as a guest sees it{% endif %} — and sets yours ₹{{ settings.benchmark_undercut|float|round|int }} under it. They raise, you raise; they cut, you cut. A room they do not sell that night gets no proposal and says so, rather than being priced against a room of a different kind. The step cap, the floor and the ceiling still apply, and a room still moves at most once per night.

{% else %}

The market figure is the median of every competitor's entry price in the same tier. Your room is not expected to match it: over the last {{ rule.HISTORY_NIGHTS }} nights the rule measures where your price usually sits against that median (say 30% above), and keeps you there — the market moves down, you move down by the same share; it moves up, you move up. Sold-out competitors (and, if you set it, a weekend) lift the price. Then the move is capped by the step, held outside the floor or ceiling, and made at most once per room per night. Percentages are of our current price for the room.

{% endif %}
