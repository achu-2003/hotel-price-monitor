{# THE SWITCH BELONGS WITH THE BUTTONS IT REPLACES. It used to sit in the rule form below, saved by "Save rule" along with nine percentages -- so the one control that lets rates move unattended was the easiest thing on the page to change by accident, and the hardest to find on purpose. Here it reads as the third choice beside Preview and Apply, which is what it is: do nothing, do it now, or do it every half hour. It saves ITSELF, immediately, to its own endpoint. Nothing else on this row is sent with it and Save rule no longer touches it. #}

Preview signs in and reads every mapped row without changing anything. Apply does the same and writes the rates marked will apply. Change a Proposed price to set your own: it is the price guests should see on the site, and Apply turns it into the RMS rate shown beside it. A price you type skips the rule's step and percentage limits, not the room's RMS floor and ceiling. Each opens the rate application in a browser here and takes about a minute.
